Generic proof patterns were adapted from OpenAI's public math repository,
commit fd4aeeb2, under the Apache License, Version 2.0:

1. lean/OAI/NumberTheory/DirichletL/LogarithmicControl.lean
   https://github.com/openai/math/blob/fd4aeeb2/lean/OAI/NumberTheory/DirichletL/LogarithmicControl.lean
   Used for holomorphic-log normalization and Borel-Caratheodory proof patterns.

2. lean/OAI/NumberTheory/DirichletL/Eisenstein/SourceMellinInversion.lean
   https://github.com/openai/math/blob/fd4aeeb2/lean/OAI/NumberTheory/DirichletL/Eisenstein/SourceMellinInversion.lean
   Used for the generic weighted Fubini and Mellin-inversion argument.

3. lean/OAI/NumberTheory/DirichletL/Hecke/PrimeDyadicContour.lean
   https://github.com/openai/math/blob/fd4aeeb2/lean/OAI/NumberTheory/DirichletL/Hecke/PrimeDyadicContour.lean
   Used for the finite-rectangle calculation; the infinite limiting proof is independent.

4. lean/OAI/NumberTheory/DirichletL/CompactProfileFamily.lean
   https://github.com/openai/math/blob/fd4aeeb2/lean/OAI/NumberTheory/DirichletL/CompactProfileFamily.lean
   Used for uniform derivative L1 and Fourier bounds on compact parameter families.

5. lean/OAI/NumberTheory/DirichletL/PrimeLogProfile.lean
   https://github.com/openai/math/blob/fd4aeeb2/lean/OAI/NumberTheory/DirichletL/PrimeLogProfile.lean

6. lean/OAI/NumberTheory/DirichletL/Mellin/InverseContour.lean
   https://github.com/openai/math/blob/fd4aeeb2/lean/OAI/NumberTheory/DirichletL/Mellin/InverseContour.lean

7. lean/OAI/NumberTheory/DirichletL/Mellin/UniformKernelBounds.lean
   https://github.com/openai/math/blob/fd4aeeb2/lean/OAI/NumberTheory/DirichletL/Mellin/UniformKernelBounds.lean
   Used for compact-parameter multiplier/Fourier estimates in Schwartz space.

Changes include new namespaces, removal of special number-field imports,
replacement of the original sieve cutoff by a Mathlib smooth bump, adaptation
to the requested Mathlib revision, explicit constants, uniformity interfaces,
and new prime-power, mask, and paired-moment assembly.
