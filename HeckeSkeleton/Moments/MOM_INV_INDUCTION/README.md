# Canonical inverse induction

This directory implements the canonical inverse recursion of Proposition 4.10
and its initial source reduction for Theorem 4.1. The original
`HeckeSkeleton.canonical_inverse_models` proof body calls
`SourceModelClosure.actual_canonical_inverse_models`,
with its original type and inputs unchanged. The old relative theorem is an
intermediate result; it is not acceptance of the original task.
The verification contract below defines acceptance. Evidence for the current
head is maintained in [PR #8](https://github.com/Rogerhu12/hecke-seven-eighths/pull/8)
and its [Actions runs](https://github.com/Rogerhu12/hecke-seven-eighths/actions/workflows/mom-inv-induction.yml).

## Implemented proof chain

The actual one-step consumer calls
`MOM_INV_STEP.CanonicalStep.exists_uniform_step`. Its input is the original
orbital parent, and its recursive premise quantifies precisely the actual
smaller pieces generated by the two Poisson transforms. The provider's
`Cih + 1` and primitive error are retained and absorbed in the numerical
recursion; the old, stronger linear `StepEnergy` is not used for this
actual adapter.

The node class stores a genuine `ColumnFamily`, frozen triple, original
slots and prime coefficients, finite label and ideal-sector row pools,
actual puncture and lengths. Its energy is defined by the original
`normalizedChildEnergy`, rather than supplied as a field. A fixed original
lambda, theta calibration, complete column pool, test support, original
prime windows, margins and norm bounds are retained in admissibility.

Backward induction first chooses the child seminorm and then obtains its
parent seminorm from the provider. A finite union handles terminal and
nonterminal tests. The selected finite order and scalar precede every
varying scale, prime list, profile and spectral parameter. The depth-zero
class is empty by strict row decrease; no estimate at that class is assumed.

For short labels the actual cube completion is split into short and long
cube variables. Long cubes are reopened by the exact divisor identity,
and the true joint profile is separated by a common Mellin integral. The
fixed annular partition shifts are accounted for in the logarithmic
capacity and margin budget. Short completed blocks use the general
reflected exponent from Lemma 4.8; the three terminal powers are derived
from conductor inequalities and canonical margins.

The initial source starts with literal ideal polynomials, original zero masks
and all original slot tuples. Its squarefree overlap, first primitive
Poisson identity and complete canonical-child reconstruction are assembled
in `SourceMarkedRawBound` and specialized in `SourceMarkedClosure`. Every
original prime weight is divided by one fixed cap before the common/residual
split; the exact full-slot scalar is restored after the actual source
estimate. The literal common-tuple count and complete column scale cancel
against the original square-root normalization.

For the unmarked seed, `SourceScaleSup` constructs one positive row majorant
for every smaller column scale of the same frozen profile.
`SourceUnmarkedComplete` uses the zero-slot canonical estimate on complete
ideal rows, and `SourceCanonicalClosure` discharges it from the two original
sieve inputs. `SourceModelClosure` combines both original obligations.
This source integration is subject to complete native compilation and all
four strict axiom gates in the verification contract below.
